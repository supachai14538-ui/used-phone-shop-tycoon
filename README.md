# 📱 Used Phone Shop Tycoon

> A business management tycoon game about buying, repairing, and selling used smartphones.

---

## 🎮 About

Used Phone Shop Tycoon is a business simulation game where players start with a small second-hand phone shop and gradually build it into a successful mobile business empire.

Players must inspect devices, negotiate with customers, repair phones, manage inventory, set selling prices, and expand their business through smart decisions.

## 🎯 Core Gameplay Loop

```text
Customer Arrives
        ↓
Inspect Phone
        ↓
Negotiate
        ↓
Buy Device
        ↓
Repair
        ↓
Store Inventory
        ↓
Customer Wants to Buy
        ↓
Set Selling Price
        ↓
Negotiate
        ↓
Sell
        ↓
Profit
        ↓
End Day
```
