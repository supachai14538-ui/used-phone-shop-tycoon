# 🗺️ DEVELOPMENT ROADMAP
# Used Phone Shop Tycoon

Version: 1.0

Status: Active Development

---

# Vision

Create a high-quality, mobile-first business simulation game where players build and manage their own second-hand phone shop.

The project follows a milestone-based development process.

Every milestone must be stable before moving to the next.

---
