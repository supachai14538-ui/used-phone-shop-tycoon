# 🔊 AUDIO BIBLE
# Used Phone Shop Tycoon

Version: 1.0

Status: Master Audio Design Guide

---

# Overview

The Audio Bible defines all sound design principles, music direction, audio categories, and implementation standards for the game.

Audio should improve immersion, reinforce player feedback, and create the feeling of running a real second-hand phone business.

Sound should never overwhelm the player.

---

# Audio Philosophy

Every sound must have a purpose.

Audio should

- Support gameplay
- Reinforce player actions
- Improve immersion
- Provide clear feedback

Silence is better than unnecessary noise.

---

Sounds should occur immediately after player actions.
