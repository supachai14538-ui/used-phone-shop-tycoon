# Design Philosophy

Colors should communicate business information clearly.

Every color must have a purpose.

Avoid decorative colors without meaning.

The player should understand the game state at a glance.

# Color Consistency Rules

One Meaning

↓

One Color

Never assign multiple meanings to the same color.

Never use random colors.

Consistency is more important than variety.

---

# Final Principle

Colors are part of the gameplay.

Players should understand the business situation instantly,

without reading every number.

Every color should help the player make faster and better decisions.
