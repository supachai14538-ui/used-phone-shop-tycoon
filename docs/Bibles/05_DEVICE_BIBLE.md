# Overview

The Device Bible defines the visual design, structure, specifications, and behavior of every phone in the game.

Every phone must follow this document.

The objective is to maintain consistency across all current and future devices.

---

# Design Philosophy

Phones are the most important business assets.

Every device should feel

- Valuable
- Unique
- Recognizable
- Collectible

Players should instantly recognize different models.
